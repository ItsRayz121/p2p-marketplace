The Solution

A marketplace that never touches your funds

RupChain replaces trust with structure: identity, on-chain proof, and algorithmic protection — while assets move peer-to-peer, off-platform.

  • Non-custodial by design — USDT and community tokens settle wallet-to-wallet; RupChain holds no balances.
  • Trade Protection engine — layered KYC (L1/L2 video), maker bonds, tier caps, reputation scoring & dispute resolution.
  • On-chain verification — every settlement carries an explorer-linked proof; delivery is provable, not promised.
  • Gas Station — buy native gas across 6+ chains instantly with auto-detection & auto-refund.
