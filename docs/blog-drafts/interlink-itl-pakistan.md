## What is InterLink, and what's the difference between ITL and ITLG?

InterLink is a mobile app that lets you mine a crypto token from your phone — no expensive equipment, no drained battery. It runs on *Proof of Personhood*: before you can fully participate, you verify that you're a real human (usually a quick facial/liveness check), which keeps bots and fake accounts out.

The part that trips everyone up is that there are **two different tokens**, and they do different jobs:

- **ITLG (InterLink Genesis Token)** — this is what you **earn by mining** in the app, through check-ins, identity verification, and referrals. It's a participation and governance token. Importantly, per InterLink's own documentation, **ITLG is not tradable until its Token Generation Event (TGE)** — until then, your balance is a claimable reward, not something you can freely sell.
- **ITL (InterLink Token)** — this is the **payment token** designed to be spent (shopping, services) and listed on exchanges. It's the token that's **actually buyable and sellable**. InterLink also allocates a large share of ITL supply to ITLG holders, which is how your mining is meant to turn into real, tradable value.

In plain terms: **you mine ITLG, and the token you buy and sell is ITL.** If someone is offering to "buy your mined tokens" today, make sure you both understand exactly which token, and which rules, apply.

## Why does InterLink's price look so confusing right now?

Because ITL is still early and ITLG isn't tradable yet, there's no single settled market price to point to. That's why you'll see one person offering $0.5 and another claiming $3 — everyone is guessing, and some are hoping you'll guess wrong.

Two mistakes to avoid while things are unsettled:

1. **Panic-selling too low.** Dumping to the first buyer for far less than others are paying hands away real work for nothing.
2. **Chasing an unrealistic high.** Waiting on a $3 buyer who may never pay — or who plans to scam you — can cost you the whole balance.

The safer approach is to trade where you can see what real deals are actually closing at, so you land in a sensible range instead of the extremes. A peer-to-peer market with a visible reference rate does that for you.

## How do you mine InterLink (ITLG)?

If you're already mining, skip ahead. If you're new, the flow inside the app is short — a handful of tasks, similar to other mining apps:

1. **Verify ITLG** — tap verify; you may see a short ad before it completes.

## How to back up your InterLink wallet the right way

This is the most important part of the whole guide, so don't skip it. If you lose access to your wallet, or someone gets your recovery phrase, your tokens are gone and nobody can get them back.

When you set up your wallet:

1. Open **Backup** in the wallet.
2. Tap **Show Recovery Phrase**.
3. Write the words down **on paper**, in order, and store them somewhere private. Don't screenshot them into a phone gallery or a cloud that could be hacked.
4. The app will ask you to confirm a few words (for example word #1, #4, and #10) to prove you saved them. Match them and your backup is complete.

That recovery phrase is the master key to your wallet. Whoever has it, owns the tokens.

## How to avoid InterLink scams

Most losses in mining-token communities come down to a handful of tricks. Watch for these:

- **"Send your recovery phrase to migrate your tokens."** Never. Migration never requires your seed phrase.
- **"Verify your wallet on this website."** Real verification happens inside the app, not on a random link.
- **A buyer offering a suspiciously high price** who wants you to send first, move to a private chat, or pay outside the protected market. Keep every trade inside the marketplace.
- **Fake "support" or "admin" DMs.** Official help never asks for your keys or password.

The single rule that prevents almost all of it: your recovery phrase and private keys stay with you, always. No legitimate service needs them.
