## How do you earn or mine VERY?

If you're already mining, skip ahead. If you're new, the loop is short:

1. **Install VeryChat and create your account.** This sets up your self-custodial wallet at the same time. Save your recovery phrase now (next section).
2. **Tap to mine.** Press the mine button about once every 12 hours. Each tap credits VERY to your balance.
3. **Raise your mining power.** Your rate multiplier climbs as your account level rises and as you invite friends who become active themselves.
4. **Claim airdrops and campaigns.** The project has run an airdrop (a fixed VERY allocation for eligible users) and periodic trading or partner campaigns. Claiming is separate from buying, and you never pay to claim a legitimate airdrop.

Mined and claimed VERY lands in your VeryChat wallet. Whether every reward is transferable straight away or partly vests over time can change with the project's tokenomics, so check the official app before you promise a buyer a set amount by a set date.

## Why is VERY's price hard to pin down right now?

Because the market is still forming. VERY is early, listings on major centralised exchanges are limited or unconfirmed, and most trading is informal and peer-to-peer. That's why one person offers a fraction of a cent and another quotes many times more. Everyone is guessing, and some are hoping you guess wrong.

Two mistakes to avoid while things are unsettled:

1. **Panic-selling too low.** Dumping your whole balance on the first buyer for far less than others are paying gives away real effort for nothing.
2. **Chasing an unrealistic high.** Holding out for a buyer at a headline number who may never pay, or who plans to scam you, can cost you the entire balance.

The safer approach is to trade where you can see what real deals are closing at. A peer-to-peer market with a **visible reference rate**, the median of recent completed trades, keeps you in a sensible range instead of at the extremes.

## How to set up and back up your VeryChat wallet

This is the most important part of the guide. If you lose access to your wallet, or someone gets your recovery phrase, your VERY is gone and nobody can recover it.

When you set up your wallet:

1. Open **Backup** (or **Security**, then **Recovery Phrase**) in the wallet.
2. Tap **Show Recovery Phrase**. It's also called a seed phrase, usually 12 words.
3. Write the words down **on paper**, in order, and store the paper somewhere private. Don't screenshot them into your phone gallery, and don't paste them into notes, chat, or cloud storage that could be hacked.
4. Confirm the words when the app asks. It will quiz you on a few positions so you know your copy is correct.
5. Optional but wise: keep a second paper copy in a different physical location.

That recovery phrase is the master key. Whoever holds it, owns the tokens.

## How to avoid VeryChat and VERY token scams

Most losses around app tokens come down to a short list of tricks. With VERY, look-alike websites are the big one.

- **Fake "Sell your $VERY" sites.** Several domains that look official run swap widgets that never pay out, or that phish your wallet. Don't connect your wallet or paste anything into a site you reached from a DM, an ad, or a comment. Check the domain against the project's official channels first.
- **"Send your recovery phrase to migrate, claim, or unlock."** Never. No migration or airdrop claim needs your seed phrase.
- **"Verify your wallet on this link."** Real actions happen inside the app, not on a random page.
- **A buyer offering a suspiciously high price** who wants you to send first, move off-platform, or pay outside the protected market. Keep everything inside the marketplace.
- **Fake "support" or "admin" DMs.** Official help never asks for your keys, password, or one-time codes.

One rule prevents almost all of this: your recovery phrase and private keys stay with you, always. No legitimate service needs them.
